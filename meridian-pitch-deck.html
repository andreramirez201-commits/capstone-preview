06 — Human Review & Success Measures
AI drafts.
A person decides.
A person sends.

No proposal or follow-up reaches a client unreviewed. Three numbers track whether the workflow is actually working — all three are targets today, not measured results.

Proposal drafting time
14 hrs→7 hrs target
Time to first follow-up
5+ days→<24 hrs proposed team target
Proposal win rate
Declining→Tracked monthly — no result claimed yet

Drafting time: clock starts at discovery notes, ends when the human-reviewed proposal is sent. Follow-up time: clock starts when a sent proposal goes quiet, ends when Message 1 is actually sent — not generated. Generating a message isn't sending it: a rep still reviews the draft, sets their own reminder, sends it, and records when they did.

Scope note: the brief's "5+ days" could mean first response to a new lead, or follow-up after a proposal is sent. This workflow is scoped to post-proposal follow-up — confirmed with the team before presenting, not assumed.

07 — Responsible AI

Four guardrails, built into the workflow.

Accuracy

Every one of the five prompts is instructed to label an unknown "Needs Confirmation" instead of inventing it — verified in testing for four of five stages.

No overpromising

Human review is the safeguard here, not a separate AI check — a person reads every stage's output before approving it, specifically for invented prices, dates, or guaranteed outcomes.

Confidentiality

Only fictional or anonymized inputs go into the AI tool — never real client data.

Human sign-off

A named rep reviews and sends every output. The follow-up tracker only records a simulated timestamp locally — nothing here sends on its own.

Pilot first. Measure before we scale.
